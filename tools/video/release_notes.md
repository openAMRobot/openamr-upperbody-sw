Two videos rendered offline from recorded simulation artifacts. Nothing was re-simulated; no new mission was run. Every panel is drawn from the recorded ROS 2 bag, the candump logs of the virtual CAN buses and the run's own report JSON. Rendering scripts: branch `diag/sim-videos`, `tools/video/` (`make_all.sh` regenerates both files from the artifacts).

**Simulation only: not hardware validation, not perception validation, not safety evidence.** Profile: simulation-oracle (`pose_source: ground_truth`, `object_pose_source: ground_truth`): the Nav2 planning scene and the object and grasp poses come from Gazebo ground truth. Physics: Gazebo Harmonic with DART. Devices: host emulators on virtual CAN (ZLAC8015D drive, CTL-017 lift controller, Daly BMS, Damiao arm motors). The STM32 base controller is a host stand-in (`stm32: host`), not firmware in Renode. No command arbiter in these runs: collision_monitor is the single `/cmd_vel` writer.

### video_b_grocery.mp4: grocery warehouse demonstration (2:00, 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264, no audio)
Source: run https://github.com/openAMRobot/openamr-upperbody-sw/actions/runs/44299879412 (demo/grocery-dark-store 6170e6a, artifact grocery-demo-attempt1).
Result: **10 of 12 phase checks passed. Failed: nav_to_shelf (parked 49.7 mm from the shelf station goal, limit 40 mm; station alignment off in this run) and nav_to_packing (odometry guard: wheel odometry minus ground truth 0.907 m, guard 0.56 m; attributed to wheel slip of the Gazebo wheel collision).** Pick, transport, placement into the order tote and return to parking completed.
Left: the store camera recorded in the run (960x540, 2 frames per Gazebo second, shown at 1.25x; enlargement adds no detail). Right, by section: system overview; top-view REPLAY drawn from the bag (LiDAR `/scan_filtered` placed with the ground-truth pose, `/plan`, `/amcl_pose`, `/sil/base/odom`); lift command (`/sil/lift/command`), decoded vcan_aux frames and TPDO1 position feedback; `/cmd_vel_nav`, `/cmd_vel_smoothed`, `/cmd_vel`; arm joints (`/arm/joint_states`), finger (`/build1/ground_truth/finger_joint_states`) and item contact force (`/build1/contacts/world_item`); device states (`/sil/*/status`), drive command vs wheel speed and the odometry guard. One camera angle only: extra Gazebo camera angles would need a new simulation run.

### video_c_engineering.mp4: engineering digital twin explained (3:21, 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264, no audio)
Source: run https://github.com/openAMRobot/openamr-upperbody-sw/actions/runs/44309791416 (integration/baseline1 d63e9ed, openamr-platform-sw 931e631, artifact baseline1-acceptance-attempt1), Baseline 1, 8 of 8 phases PASS.
