# Integrated simulation smoke, 9 Nov 2026

Simulation evidence only. Nothing here says anything about the physical robot. Numbers are properties of this simulator on one
GitHub-hosted-class Linux runner (ubuntu-24.04, ROS 2 Jazzy, Gazebo Sim 8.15 Harmonic via ros-jazzy-ros-gz 1.0.24, gz_ros2_control from apt, no GPU, headless).

## How it was run

- Workspace: this repository's `ros2/src` plus the pinned upstream commits of `ros2/src/openamrobot_upperbody_description/config/upstream_pins.yaml`
  (openamr-platform-sw `131e500`, openarm_description `14ff67b`, openarm_ros2 `4e837e1`) cloned under `src/upstream/`. `colcon build --symlink-install --packages-up-to openamrobot_upperbody_bringup openamrobot_upperbody_gazebo`.
- Launch: `ros2 launch openamrobot_upperbody_bringup robot_sim.launch.py headless:=true` (DART, the default engine; base plate bp000, column dold_v4_350, hp30, bt10).
- Numbers: `scripts/sim_smoke_measure.py` against the live launch; test: `test/test_integrated_sim_smoke.py`.
- Nav2 (`nav2:=true`) was not run: the navigation2 packages are not installed on this runner. See the report.

## Motion, against the simulator's own pose

| move | commanded | /odom | ground truth (gz dynamic_pose) |
|---|---|---|---|
| forward at 0.2 m/s | 1.0 m | 0.98 m | 0.006 m |
| rotate at 0.4 rad/s | 90 deg | 84.5 deg | 0.0001 deg |
| lift to L1175 (0.175 m) | goal succeeds, 14.7 s sim time | lift_joint 0.0 | not read |
| lift to L1350 (0.350 m) | goal succeeds, 14.6 s sim time | lift_joint 0.0 | not read |
| both arms to `raised` (joint4 0.8 rad), then `park` | success, 5.6 s each | final error 5e-5 rad | not read |

The base and the lift do not move in the physics, while the odometry and the lift's trajectory goal report success. The existing
G01 test checks odometry only, which is why this was not seen. The same result was obtained with the description before the 8 Nov
mass and collision commits of PR #48 (`2ca7392`: odom 0.96 m, ground truth 0.000003 m), so those commits are not the cause. With
`physics_engine:=gz-physics-bullet-featherstone-plugin` the base moved 0.22 m in ground truth against 1.68 m in odom.

**Update 10 Nov 2026: diagnosed and fixed; see `docs/sim_smoke/2026-11-10-ground-truth-fix.md`.** The paragraph below is kept as it was
written on 9 Nov. Candidates (1) and (3) were the causes.

Not diagnosed to a cause: the wheels turn (wheel joint at 2.95 rad after the 90 deg turn) and the chassis stays. The lift is not held by its
limits (effort 800 N, 0.018 m/s) and the model's inertial total is 35.6 kg. Candidate causes to check, in this order: (1) wheel contact
in the combined model (wheel collision radius against the 0.100 m DiffDrive radius, the wheel friction written as `<ode>` tags that DART may
not read); (2) the upper-body collision geometry resting on something at spawn (the model sits at z = 0.002 and pitch 0.0047 rad at rest);
(3) the gz_ros2_control lift joint interface: the controller accepts the goal but `lift_joint` never leaves 0, which looks like an
interface that is not applied to the physics (check the command interface type against the plugin).

## Other findings

- The simulated IMU is on `/imu`; the real robot publishes `/imu/data_raw` (D-10). This repository now bridges it on the real name
  (`config/gz_bridge_imu_data_raw.yaml`). The clean fix is one line in platform-sw's `openamrobot_gazebo/config/gz_bridge.yaml`
  (`ros_topic_name: "imu/data_raw"`), after which the extra bridge can go.
- `ros2 control` needs `ros-jazzy-ros2controlcli`; the launch does not.
- The world step is 1 ms and the controller manager updates at 100 Hz; gz_ros2_control warns about it at startup (platform-sw / description owners).
